CALL EMERGENCY IMMEDIATELY (ABA SEVERE CRITERIA)!

115

Vietnam National Medical Emergency

Universal 4-Step Pre-Hospital Actions (Prof. Le The Trung / WHO):

1. STOP: Extinguish flames (Stop, Drop & Roll), isolate heat source, disconnect power.
2. COOL (Hypothermia Warning): Cool with gentle water for 5-10 minutes ONLY if freshly injured. Prolonged cold water is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN (triggers deadly hypothermic cardiac arrest).
3. REMOVE (No Forced Peeling): Cut away tight garments, rings, and belts. If fabric is melted or fused to skin, cut around it: NEVER tear off adhered cloth.
4. COVER & WARM: Cover open wounds loosely with a clean dry bedsheet. Pile thick, warm dry blankets over patient's body to prevent shock.

Critical Anatomical Zone Directives (Regions 1 to 9):

• Region 1 (Head, Face, Neck & Inhalation): Singed nasal hairs, hoarseness, or soot in sputum = threatened airway. Sit upright (90°). STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to place cling film over face.
• Region 2 & 3 (Arms, Forearms & Hands): Check distal pulse & warmth. Lay sheets loosely along limb (NEVER spiral wrap). Elevate hand/limb ≥ 15-20 cm above heart. Splint hand in position of function.
• Region 4 (Chest & Abdomen - Cuirass Burn): Semi-Fowler (30-45°) with knees slightly flexed to maximize lung expansion. DO NOT submerge in cold bath.
• Region 5 (Back & Buttocks): Transport strictly prone (face-down) or on side. DO NOT allow patient to lie supine.
• Region 6 & 7 (Legs & Feet): Check dorsalis pedis pulse. Elevate 30°. STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to permit walking.
• Region 8 (Major Joints): Maintain in found position. DO NOT forcefully extend or bend contracted joints.
• Region 9 (Perineum & Genitalia): ACUTE URINARY RETENTION ALERT: Urethral edema blocks urination. DO NOT give large oral fluids (prevents bladder rupture). Requires urgent emergency Foley catheterization.
• STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: DO NOT give oral food or fluids. DO NOT debride charred eschar.
